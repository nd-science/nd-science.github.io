Nataliia Dolgova

Dr. phil. (magna cum laude), Cand. Sc., TVET Researcher

Europa-Universität Flensburg ORCID: 0000-0003-0816-2103 | Scopus: 57202091034

I study how adults develop complex professional skills, how TVET educators are prepared and which conditions shape the perceived effectiveness of online learning. My work connects research, teaching, programme design and support for educational change.

Key facts: Dr. phil., Europa-Universität Flensburg, magna cum laude; Candidate of Economic Sciences; professional work since 2011; 40+ programmes; 2,000+ educators; 100+ publications; presentations at Russian and international professional events.

Core areas include vocational education, educator preparation, digital transformation, online learning, competence frameworks, human capital, and education–industry collaboration.
