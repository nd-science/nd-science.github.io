2. Purposes of Data Processing

Personal data is processed exclusively for the purposes of:

3. Categories of Data Processed

When you submit an enquiry, the Controller may collect and process:

  • name and professional title;
  • email address;
  • institution or organisation name;
  • project type, target audience, timeline, and scope;
  • accompanying commentary and project brief description voluntarily provided by the user.

4. Processing Terms and Data Retention

4.1. Data processing is carried out using secure automated and manual workflows.

4.2. Personal data is never sold or transferred to third parties, except where required by law or strictly necessary technical services that protect the enquiry form from automated submissions.

4.3. Data is retained only for as long as required to fulfil the collaboration inquiry or until the user requests its erasure.

4.4. Cloudflare Turnstile protects the form by checking technical signals needed to distinguish people from bots. It does not receive the enquiry text; see the Cloudflare Privacy Policy ↗.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, rectification of, or erasure of your personal data at any time by contacting: n.e.dolgova@mail.ru.
