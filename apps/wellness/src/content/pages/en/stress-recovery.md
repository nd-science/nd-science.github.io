A structured four-week personal programme designed to restore energy reserves, reduce chronic stress, overcome burnout, and establish a reliable self-regulation system under demanding workloads.

### Programme Structure

1. **Week 1. Assessment and Stabilisation:** Auditing current load, identifying key depletion triggers, and restoring autonomic nervous system balance through foundational breathing and sleep protocols.
2. **Week 2. Physiological Micro-Practices:** Integrating short self-regulation practices (breathwork, gentle qigong movement) into daily work without overloading your schedule.
3. **Week 3. Cognitive Resilience:** Addressing cognitive habits, perfectionism, anxiety, and the inner critic using CBT and ACT evidence-based tools.
4. **Week 4. Anchoring Your Personal Protocol:** Establishing an individualized toolkit and self-support algorithms to maintain clarity and energy over the long term.

### Format and Terms

- 4 individual 60-minute online sessions (once a week).
- Personalised audio and text materials with micro-practices for independent use.
- Asynchronous messaging support and feedback on business days.
- Fee for four weeks of support: €600.

Before starting, we clarify your request in an initial consultation or preliminary exchange, agree on a schedule, and establish criteria for your expected progress.
