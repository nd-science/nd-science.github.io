This section features resources on stress physiology, sustainable habits, professional transitions and self-regulation. They help introduce the methodology and practices, but do not replace an individual consultation.

### Public channels and updates

- **Telegram channel [“Natalia Dolgova | Breathing & Neurointegration”](https://t.me/natalicompass)** — regular reflections, practical insights into stress physiology, breathing techniques and sustainable pacing.
- **“Healthy Breathing” Practice Club** — group practice cycles and guided sessions. Registration is open on the [waitlist form](https://forms.yandex.ru/u/6aabc44f90fa7b004ca6d189).

### Focus themes

- **Stress physiology & recovery**: how the autonomic nervous system responds to sustained pressure and which somatic markers indicate fatigue.
- **Breathing patterns**: the relationship between everyday breathing habits, anxiety levels, sleep quality and cognitive resilience.
- **Neurointegration protocols**: actionable micro-routines to transition smoothly between focus, action and restorative rest.
- **Sustainable habit architecture**: why willpower-based changes often stall and how to embed somatic practices into demanding schedules without cognitive overload.
