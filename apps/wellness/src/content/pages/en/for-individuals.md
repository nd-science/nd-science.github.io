People come to me with everyday stress, professional burnout, overload, difficulty relaxing or sleeping, reduced concentration, relocation adjustment and career transitions.

The first meeting lasts 60 minutes. We meet, review the working terms, clarify the request and previous experience, define a possible goal and agree on a next step. Follow-up meetings last 50–60 minutes. You can begin with one consultation or choose a five-session package.

Prices are €100 online and €120 in person; the five-session package is €500 online and €600 in person. Support between sessions is provided only when it is part of the selected format.

I work with adults aged 18+. Online meetings are available worldwide where permitted by applicable law; in-person meetings take place in Belgrade. I do not take child, family, clinical or crisis cases.
