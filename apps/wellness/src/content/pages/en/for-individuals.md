People reach out to me with emotional and professional burnout, chronic stress, overload, difficulty relaxing or sleeping, impostor syndrome, relocation adjustments and career transitions.

### Common Areas of Focus:
- **Emotional and professional workplace burnout:** persistent depletion where standard rest no longer restores energy; loss of engagement, growing cynicism, and procrastination on key projects.
- **Chronic stress and cognitive overload:** difficulty disconnecting from work in the evenings, elevated anxiety, physical tension, and sleep disruption.
- **Impostor syndrome and self-doubt:** feeling like an "accidental success", fear of not meeting expectations after a promotion, and harsh self-criticism discounting past achievements.
- **Scattered attention and loss of direction:** struggling to prioritize, navigating task chaos, and feeling like you are operating purely on momentum.
- **Relocation and life transitions:** migration stress, adapting to new linguistic and cultural settings, and establishing fresh personal or professional anchors.
- **Mastering self-regulation skills:** teaching your nervous system to alternate between focused action and restorative ease without self-coercion.

An individual consultation lasts 60 minutes. We meet, review the working terms, clarify your request and previous experience, define a realistic goal and agree on a next step. Follow-up meetings last 50–60 minutes. You can begin with a single consultation or choose a five-session package for structured self-regulation work.

Prices are €100 online and €120 in person; the five-session package is €500 online and €600 in person. Corporate workshops and team wellbeing sessions are also available for organisations (details in the For organisations section). Support between sessions is provided only when it is part of the selected format.

The practice is dedicated to individual work with adult clients aged 18 and older. Online consultations are available worldwide (regular work with clients in London, Barcelona, Berlin, Limassol, Tbilisi, Yerevan, Moscow, and Belgrade); regular in-person sessions take place in Belgrade (and occasionally in London during visits by prior arrangement). I do not work with children or adolescents, nor do I take family, clinical, or crisis cases.
